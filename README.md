# AI Hustle Generator for Nigerians

Stop wondering what business to start with your money. Let AI help you.

The AI Hustle Generator is a free web tool that helps Nigerians turn a small budget and a skill into real business ideas. Enter your budget and skill, and it will generate 3 profitable hustles with a capital breakdown, step-by-step plan, and profit estimate.

**Live Demo:** https://bellosamadwork-dev.github.io/ai-hustle-generator/

---

## Features

- **AI-Powered Ideas:** Generates 3 unique and realistic business ideas based on your budget and skill
- **Built for Nigeria:** Ideas designed for the Nigerian market - low capital, high demand, phone-first
- **Capital Breakdown:** See exactly how your money will be spent
- **Step-by-Step Action Plan:** Not just ideas, but what to do from Day 1 to Day 30
- **Profit Estimate:** See the expected monthly profit for each idea
- **Mobile-First Design:** Works perfectly on your phone, no laptop needed
- **Modern UI:** Clean, fast, and easy to use
- **100% Free:** No sign-up, no payment, just generate

## Tech Stack

- **HTML5** - Structure
- **Tailwind CSS** - Styling and Responsive Design
- **JavaScript (Vanilla JS)** - Logic and API handling
- **DeepSeek AI API** - AI Idea Generation Engine
- **GitHub Pages** - Free Hosting and Deployment

## How To Use

1. Open the live link: https://bellosamadwork-dev.github.io/ai-hustle-generator/
2. Enter your **Budget** - e.g. N20,000, N50,000, N100,000
3. Enter your **Skill** - e.g. Cooking, Graphic Design, Phone Repair, No skill yet
4. Select your **Location** (optional) - Lagos, Abuja, etc.
5. Click **"Generate My Hustle Ideas"**
6. Wait 5-10 seconds and AI will generate 3 ideas for you
7. Pick one and start

## Example

**Input:** Budget: N20,000 | Skill: Cooking
**Output:**
1. Small Chops Business - Capital: N18,500 | Profit: N40k - N70k/month
2. Food Delivery for Students - Capital: N15,000 | Profit: N30k/month
3. Bulk Snacks Reselling - Capital: N20,000 | Profit: N25k/month
